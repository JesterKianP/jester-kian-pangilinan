How this preview is calculated

Products and quantities follow sql/03_seed_data.sql. Unit cost is 5 + ((product ID × 17) mod 420) + ((product ID mod 4) × 0.25). Reorder level is 5 + (product ID mod 26). Quantities are zero for multiples of 29, half the reorder level rounded down for multiples of 13, and otherwise 24 + ((product ID × 19) mod 180). Inventory value is quantity × unit cost. This preview is read-only; filters and exports run in your browser.
