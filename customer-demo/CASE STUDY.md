# Customer Records

A browser-based customer workspace for entering, updating, finding, and handing off structured customer records.

## Task

Keep customer details and order statuses consistent while reducing duplicate entry.

## Workflow

Enter or import customer records, review validation messages, search and filter the list, correct records, and export a CSV handoff.

## Quality controls

Required name and email fields, email-format validation, case-insensitive duplicate prevention, and spreadsheet-safe CSV export.

## Review the work

Open Projects/01 Customer Records/index.html. Add a fictional customer, try the same email twice, filter by status, then export the results.

## Scope

Browser-local demonstration with fictional sample data. Accounts and payments are simulated. No production usage or typing-speed claim is made.
